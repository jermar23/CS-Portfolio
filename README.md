My Computer Science program portfolio for SNHU.

## CS 250: Software Development Lifecycle

### How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by understanding what the user wants to accomplish and identifying the features that will meet those needs. In my SNHU Travel project, user stories helped break larger requirements into smaller, manageable tasks. They also helped me understand which features were important to the user and how to determine whether those features met the requirements.

### How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

I approach program development by breaking work into smaller steps, understanding the requirements, and testing features throughout development. Through CS 250, I learned how Agile and Scrum allow a team to adapt when requirements change. In the future, I would like to use sprint planning, regular communication, sprint reviews, and retrospectives to stay organized, gather feedback, and improve my work.

### What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member means communicating clearly, completing assigned tasks, listening to feedback, and being willing to help others. The SNHU Travel project helped me understand the importance of keeping requirements, development, and testing aligned when a project changes. A good team member takes responsibility for their work, asks questions when something is unclear, and works with others toward a shared goal.
